Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Draft for client review. This page is a standard template and must be reviewed and approved by Brigitte Rose and its legal advisor before launch.

1. Information We Collect

When you place an order, contact us or sign up for our newsletter, we may collect your name, email address, shipping and billing address, phone number and payment details. We also collect limited technical information such as browser type and pages visited to help us improve the site.

2. How We Use It

  • To process and deliver your orders and send order confirmations.
  • To respond to your enquiries.
  • To send newsletters and offers, only where you have opted in. You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To improve our products, website and customer experience.

3. Payment Security

Payment information is processed by our payment provider over an encrypted connection. Brigitte Rose does not store full card numbers on its servers.

4. Sharing

We share personal information only with service providers who help us run the business (such as shipping carriers and payment processors), and only to the extent needed to provide those services. We do not sell your personal information.

5. Cookies & Local Storage

This site uses browser storage to remember the contents of your bag and wishlist between visits. You can clear this at any time in your browser settings.

6. Your Rights

California residents have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of their personal information under the CCPA. To make a request, contact us at the address below.

7. Children

Our site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

8. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with a revised date.

9. Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to ex@domain.com, Brigitte Rose, Pacific Palisades, California.
