Meet the Founder

Brigitte Pylen, Founder & CEO

Brigitte Pylen is a 17-year-old entrepreneur and the Founder and CEO of Brigitte Rose. With a strong passion for business, fashion, and bringing her ideas to life, she founded the brand with a vision to combine beautiful designs, exceptional comfort, and her signature touch of sparkle.

Having grown up in Pacific Palisades, Brigitte experienced firsthand the devastation of the Palisades fire and the challenges of being displaced from her home. This experience gave her a deeper appreciation for the importance of comfort, especially during life's most difficult and uncertain moments. She wanted her pajama sets to offer not only beautiful designs but also a sense of warmth, comfort, and familiarity that people could turn to when they needed it most.

For Brigitte, entrepreneurship is an opportunity to combine her creativity with her love for business and make her ideas a reality. She created and built Brigitte Rose entirely on her own, bringing her vision to life through her creativity, dedication, and hard work. She personally manages every aspect of the business, from designing her pajama sets and working directly with manufacturers to making the decisions that shape its future. Brigitte Rose represents the time, care, and dedication she has invested in every step of building her business.

Giving back holds a special place in Brigitte's heart. Her experience with the Palisades fire inspired her to use Brigitte Rose as a way to bring hope and support to communities affected by the devastating wildfires. A portion of proceeds supports the Los Angeles County Relief Fund, contributing to recovery efforts and helping families and communities rebuild their lives.

Above all, Brigitte hopes to bring happiness and comfort to others when they need it most, one pajama set at a time.

Our Story

About Brigitte Rose

Brigitte Rose is a pajama brand based in Pacific Palisades, Los Angeles, born from a passion for fashion, beautiful designs, and exceptional comfort. With a signature touch of sparkle, the brand brings together timeless style and thoughtful details to create pajama sets that are as special as the moments spent wearing them.

Every pajama set is thoughtfully created, reflecting the dedication behind Brigitte Rose. Designed to be enjoyed both at home and beyond, our pajamas are made to bring beauty and joy to everyday life.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Our mission is to make a meaningful difference in people's lives by bringing happiness and comfort through our designs. We recognize the importance of feeling comfortable, especially during periods of rest, recovery, and uncertainty. We hope our pajama sets provide a sense of ease and reassurance during these moments, while our signature sparkle adds a touch of joy and positivity. Above all, we hope Brigitte Rose brings a sense of happiness and comfort to others, both in their everyday lives and during the moments when they need it most.

Our Vision

Our vision is to establish Brigitte Rose as a brand recognized not only for its designs, but also for the positive impact it makes on others. We envision a future where Brigitte Rose continues to evolve while remaining true to the passion and purpose behind its creation. We hope to inspire delight through what we create and make a meaningful contribution to the lives of others through what we do.

Giving Back

Giving Back

Giving back holds a special place in the heart of Brigitte Rose. Inspired by the founder's personal experience with the Palisades fire, supporting families and communities affected by the devastating Los Angeles wildfires is a meaningful part of our brand's purpose.

Through Brigitte Rose, we are proud to contribute to the recovery of those affected, with the hope of helping families rebuild their homes and lives. A portion of proceeds supports the Los Angeles County Relief Fund, helping with recovery and rebuilding efforts following the Palisades and Eaton fires in the Pacific Palisades and Altadena communities.
