About Us

Comfort, made beautiful.

Brigitte Rose was born from a simple idea: pajamas shouldn't just be comfortable, they should make you feel beautiful. As a new luxury sleepwear brand, we design pajama sets using premium fabrics and elegant details for women who want their everyday moments to feel special.

Meet the Founder

Brigitte Pylen, Founder & Creative Director

Brigitte Pylen is a 17-year-old entrepreneur and the Founder and Creative Director of Brigitte Rose. With a strong passion for business, fashion, and creating something meaningful, she founded Brigitte Rose after her experience with the Palisades fire showed her how important comfort can be during uncertain moments.

Inspired by both her personal experience and the role comfort can play during recovery, Brigitte created the brand to bring together comfort, glamour, sparkle, and purpose. She is personally involved in every part of Brigitte Rose, from the designs and fabrics to the branding and overall vision.

Through the brand, she hopes to turn part of her own story into something positive by bringing comfort and confidence to others while giving back through a portion of proceeds supporting fire-relief efforts benefiting Pacific Palisades and Altadena.

Professional Background

Background & Qualifications

Brigitte combines hands-on experience building Brigitte Rose with a growing background in business and entrepreneurship.

She is directly involved in product design, fabric and color selection, manufacturer communication, sample development and revisions, pricing, branding, marketing, and overall business strategy. She has also completed the Girls With Impact Business & Leadership Academy and additional coursework focused on business and sustainability. Her experience developing Brigitte Rose from concept to launch has given her firsthand experience in both the creative and business sides of building a brand.

Brigitte Rose creates luxury pajama sets where comfort meets glamour. Designed for rest, recovery, travel, lounging, and everything in between, each set pairs incredibly soft comfort with sparkling details and statement touches that make every moment feel more special.

Brigitte Rose was founded in 2024, with product development and production beginning in 2025.

“Brigitte Rose brings beauty and sparkle to the moments when comfort matters most.”
Brigitte Pylen
Mission & Vision

Make comfort feel beautiful, meaningful, and special.

Brigitte Rose's mission is to make comfort feel beautiful, meaningful, and special. I want every set to bring together softness, confidence, glamour, and a little sparkle, especially during the moments when comfort matters most, whether that is rest, recovery, travel, or simply taking time for yourself.

The values behind Brigitte Rose are comfort, confidence, individuality, quality, and giving back. Every detail is meant to feel intentional, from the fabrics and designs to the experience of wearing each set.

Most importantly, I want the brand to have a purpose beyond the product itself. A portion of proceeds from Brigitte Rose will be donated to the Los Angeles County Relief Fund to support fire relief efforts benefiting Pacific Palisades and Altadena. Giving back is deeply connected to the story of Brigitte Rose and to my hope that the brand can bring a little comfort, beauty, and sparkle to others while helping communities rebuild.

Why Choose Brigitte Rose

Sleepwear built around comfort, confidence, personality, and meaning.

Brigitte Rose was created to make comfort feel just as special as style. Each set is designed with rest and recovery in mind, while still feeling glamorous, elevated, and unique enough to wear beyond the bedroom.

From incredibly soft fabrics to bold prints, sparkling sequin details, and thoughtful design touches, every piece is meant to stand out while still being something you'll reach for again and again.

Brigitte Rose also has a purpose beyond the product, with a portion of proceeds supporting fire-relief efforts benefiting Pacific Palisades and Altadena. It is sleepwear built around comfort, confidence, personality, and meaning.
